# Corridor: Czechia

URLs checked: 2026-11-18.

This corridor covers people who live in Czechia, read English or Ukrainian rather than Czech, and have to deal with Czech
offices. It includes non-EU residents on long-term visas and permits, EU citizens, and Ukrainians with temporary
protection. The table lists the 21 forms and official letters such a person is most likely to receive or have to fill
in, ranked by how commonly a newcomer meets them. Each row gives the issuing authority, the channel, and an official URL
with its verification status. "Verified" means the URL was fetched on the check date and is the page for that item, or
an official page that explains it. "Verified (search)" means a search result showed that exact URL but it was not
fetched.

## Ranking basis

The ranking is my judgment. No official statistics count individual forms or letters, such as how many address reports
or prohlášení are filed each year, so the order within the table is not measured. The judgment rests on who must deal
with each item:

- Letters that reach nearly everyone rank highest (rows 1 and 6).
- Forms that every employee, insured person or TP holder must handle come next (rows 2 to 5).
- Residence-procedure items that only non-EU applicants meet follow (rows 7 to 11).
- Items for subgroups rank lowest: self-employed people (rows 13, 18 and 19), people in need or out of work (rows 14
  and 15), and EU citizens (row 20, which is optional).

The size of each group comes from Ministry of the Interior statistics:

- **31 Oct 2026:** 1,153,486 foreigners were registered. Of these, 393,969 held temporary protection (393,268 of them
  Ukrainian), 343,761 held temporary residence (přechodný pobyt) and 415,756 held permanent residence. Ukrainians
  totalled 620,717 across the three categories; I added the three Ukraine rows myself. Source: monthly table "stav k
  31. 10. 2026" on https://mv.gov.cz/cizinci-s-povolenym-pobytem---prehled-za-rok-2026
- **31 Dec 2025:** 1,131,197 foreigners were registered, including 393,056 active temporary protections. About 351,000
  people used the extension that ran to the end of March 2026, and 89,358 temporary protections were issued in 2025.
  Source: MV ČR, Čtvrtletní zpráva o migraci IV. 2025,
  https://archiv.mv.gov.cz/volby/soubor/ctvrtletni-zprava-o-migraci-iv-2025.aspx
- **End of April 2026:** 56,503 people had expressed interest in the special long-term residence for TP holders
  (zvláštní dlouhodobý pobyt). Source: https://mv.gov.cz/migrace/o-zvlastni-dlouhodoby-pobyt-ma-v-roce-2026-zajem-56-tisic-osob

These numbers support ranking the TP extension high: it is a yearly, mandatory step for about 394,000 people, roughly a
third of all registered foreigners.

## Ukrainian readers

- **The temporary protection extension is yearly** and has two steps: online registration with appointment booking,
  then an in-person visit for a new visa sticker. In the 2026 round, registration closed on 15 Mar 2026, and the
  sticker had to be collected by 30 Sep 2026. The extension ran to 31 Mar 2027.
- **The next round runs to 31 Mar 2028.** An MV article of 4 Aug 2026 says it will run the same way, and that the
  dates will be published on ipc.gov.cz
  (https://archiv.mv.gov.cz/clanek/evropska-unie-zprisnuje-podminky-pro-udeleni-docasne-ochrany-obcanum-ukrajiny.aspx).
- **New applications from 5 Aug 2026:** men of military-service age who have not met their military obligations no
  longer qualify. This condition does not apply to extensions (same MV article).
- **TP holders report a new address within three working days** if they will stay there more than 15 days. Ordinary
  long-term residents have 30 days. An address left registered at an MV office for more than 90 days ends the
  protection. Source: ipc.gov.cz Ukrainian TP page, https://ipc.gov.cz/uk/tymchasovyy-zakhyst/
- **The state pays health insurance for 90 days after protection is granted** (same page). After that, adults of
  working age need another basis, such as employment or registration with Úřad práce (rows 3 and 15).
- **Humanitarian benefit (row 14):** repeat applications are online only and cannot be backdated. The application form
  is available as a Ukrainian-language PDF on mpsv.gov.cz.
- **Ukrainian-language official content was verified at these sources:**
  - ipc.gov.cz/uk/: the TP pages, the 2026 extension page, and the special long-term residence page
    (https://ipc.gov.cz/uk/zvlastni-dlouhodoby-pobyt-ua/).
  - The ipc.gov.cz forms page: a bilingual Czech-Ukrainian employee-card notice of a change of employer.
  - mpsv.gov.cz: the humanitarian benefit form.
  - VZP's pomocukrajine.vzp.cz pages appeared in Ukrainian in search results but were not fetched.

## Out of scope

Deliberately left out:

- Permanent residence and citizenship applications (not newcomer items)
- Asylum and international protection
- Family-reunification and Blue Card specifics
- Visa applications at embassies abroad
- Vehicle and driving-licence forms, school enrolment and birth registration
- Property tax and VAT
- Documents issued by bailiffs themselves
- Private letters (landlord, bank, energy, telecom)
