Privacy policy

Last changed on 17 November 2026. This policy covers the Distill web app and the Distill extension for Chrome.

In short

  • What you type into a form stays on your device. Distill never sends it anywhere and never saves it online.
  • The AI runs on your device first, with Gemini Nano, the model built into Chrome.
  • The extension you can download uses only the model on your device. It sends nothing to the cloud and saves nothing online.
  • No analytics. No ads. No tracking. We do not sell or share anything.

Your typed answers

When Distill guides you through a form, the answers you type, and any help with them (suggestions, checks), stay on your device. Suggestions and checks run only with the AI model on your device. If your device has no model, Distill tells you so and you type your answers yourself. Nothing is sent to the cloud instead.

Your place in a form guide and your answers are kept in your browser while you work, so a page reload does not lose them. They are cleared when you close the tab, or when you choose "Forget my answers".

The AI and where it runs

Distill uses Firebase AI Logic from Google. It first tries Gemini Nano, the AI model built into Chrome, which runs on your computer: then the page never leaves your device. Each result shows where it was made: "On this device" or "In the cloud".

In the web app, and in an extension you build yourself, Distill can ask the cloud (Google's Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic) when your device cannot help. Then it sends the text of the page, or the questions and labels of a form: the structure, never your answers. Google handles that text under the Firebase data processing terms.

The extension you can download never asks the cloud. If the model on your device is not ready, it shows the page as written, a simple guide for forms, and the reading view instead.

Firestore is Google's cloud database. Only you can read the documents under your anonymous account. They never hold an answer you typed: the app refuses to save one, and so do the database rules.

Proving the app is genuine

The web app uses Firebase App Check with Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise, so only the real app can use the cloud AI. reCAPTCHA looks at information about your browser and device to do this, under Google's privacy policy. The extension you download does not use App Check or reCAPTCHA, because it never calls the cloud.

The web app, and an extension you build yourself, also read a few app settings from Firebase Remote Config (for example, which cloud model to use). For this, Firebase gives your browser a random installation id. The extension you download does not do this.

Pages the extension reads

The extension reads a page only when you open Distill on it, or on sites you allow it to read. It reads the page text and the form fields to make the reading view and the form guide. It also reads the page's colours and fonts to match its look. It does not keep a history of the pages you visit.

Connecting an AI agent (Handrail)

If you choose to connect a helper program on your computer, an AI agent you run can read the page in Distill and propose answers. Nothing changes in a form until you accept it. What the agent does with what it reads is up to the agent you chose and its own terms. Distill asks for your permission before it connects.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, no usage tracking, no advertising.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information.
  • We do not use your information for anything except making Distill work for you.

Like any website, the web app is served by a hosting service (Firebase Hosting from Google), which receives normal request details such as your IP address to send you the page.

Removing your information

Your typed answers are only in your browser: close the tab or choose "Forget my answers". To remove settings and form guides kept on your device, clear this site's data in your browser, or remove the extension. Data under an anonymous account in the cloud is not linked to your name. To ask us to remove it, write to us (see below).

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. Distill is open source. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/xkazm04/distill.
