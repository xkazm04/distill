Riverside Park reopens with a new playground

Updated 10 minutes ago

After eighteen months of work, Riverside Park opened its gates again on Saturday morning. Families queued at the north entrance before eight, and the first children were on the new climbing frame a few minutes later.

The new playground has a water garden, a rope bridge and a quiet corner with sand tables for the youngest. The paths are now step-free all the way from the bus stop on Mill Street to the boathouse.

The council paid for most of the work, with help from the Friends of Riverside Park, who raised money for the benches and the new trees along the river. Volunteers will run a café in the old boathouse at weekends.

Opening hours: every day from 6:00 to 22:00. Dogs on a lead, please.

The park keeper asked visitors to keep to the paths while the new grass grows, and said the meadow by the river will open in June. Bicycles are welcome on the riverside path but not in the playground.
