Visiting the Harbour City Library

The central library on Quay Street is free for everyone who lives, works or studies in Harbour City.

Opening hours

From Monday to Friday the doors are open from 9 in the morning until 8 in the evening. On Saturdays the library opens at 10 in the morning and closes at 2 in the afternoon. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Borrowing

With a free card you can borrow up to twelve books at a time for three weeks. You can renew a loan twice online, unless another reader has reserved the book.

A late return costs 20 cents a day for each item, up to a limit of 5 dollars. Children's books never have a late fee.

Getting a card

Bring a photo ID and a letter or bill that shows your address in the city. The card is ready in a few minutes at the front desk.

Wi-Fi, computers and printing are available to every card holder, and printing costs 10 cents a page.
