Five spring walks in the valley

By the visitor centre · 12 March

Spring comes early to the lower valley. By the end of March the meadows along the river are full of wild garlic, and the path from the old mill to the chapel is dry enough for ordinary shoes. The full list of routes gives the length and the climb of each walk.

The second walk climbs through the beech wood to the lookout tower. It takes about two hours, and the tower is open at weekends from April. Children under ten climb the tower free of charge.

The visitor centre is open daily from nine to five.
Tip: the bus from the town square stops at the old mill every hour on Saturdays.

The third walk follows the old railway line, flat all the way, and is suitable for pushchairs and wheelchairs.
