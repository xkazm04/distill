The night sky this month

Jupiter rises in the east soon after sunset and is the brightest point in the sky all month. Through binoculars its four largest moons show as a short line of faint stars on either side of the planet.

The Geminid meteor shower peaks in the middle of the month. Away from street lights, more than a hundred meteors an hour can be seen after midnight, and the moon sets early enough not to wash them out.

The observatory on the hill opens its telescopes to the public on clear Friday evenings. Booking is not needed, but warm clothes are.
