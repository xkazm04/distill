Harbour ferry timetable doubles after 18% rise in riders

By Mara Lindqvist · Published 14 March 2023 · Updated 2 November 2026

The harbour ferry will run every ten minutes at rush hour from January, twice as often as now, the transport authority said on Monday.

Ridership on the three ferry lines rose by 18 percent last year to 4.2 million trips, according to the authority's annual report. The busiest crossing, from Old Quay to the university, now carries 9,000 passengers on a weekday.

The extra sailings will cost about $14 million a year, of which the city will pay two thirds. Two new electric boats ordered in 2024 arrive in December and will cut the fleet's fuel use by 30 percent.

"People have voted with their feet," said the authority's director, Tomas Berg, after the board meeting.

Critics say the money would be better spent on the bus network, where delays have grown by 12% since 2022. The authority says buses and ferries serve different trips and both will get more money next year.

What changes for riders

Tickets stay at the same price until at least the summer. Monthly passes bought before 31 December are valid on all three lines without a surcharge.
