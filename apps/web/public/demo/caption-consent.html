New opening hours of the county library from January

From the first of January the county library opens one hour earlier on weekdays. The reading room keeps its hours, and the children's section opens on Saturday mornings for the first time. The change follows a survey of four hundred readers held last autumn.

Readers can return books at the drop box by the main door at any time. Reservations made online are kept for five working days, after which the book goes back on the shelf for the next reader. The library asks readers to renew loans before the due date to avoid the small fine.

The library also lends e-book readers for two weeks at a time. A reader's card is enough to borrow one, and the staff at the desk explain how to load books onto it. Courses for older readers on using the online catalogue begin in February.

The library's cafe on the ground floor reopens after its renovation, with longer opening hours on Thursdays when the evening lectures take place. Lecture tickets are free but must be booked in advance, as the hall holds only eighty people.
