River town opens its new bridge after four years of work

By a staff reporter · A demo page of the Distill project; the town, the people and the numbers are made up.

The new crossing over the Example River opened on Saturday morning. The bridge cost 48 million euros, about 6 million more than the town council planned in 2022. According to the council, around 21,000 cars a day are expected to use it once the old ferry stops running.

Mayor Jana Example said at the opening that "this bridge cuts the trip to the hospital from forty minutes to twelve." The regional transport office confirmed that two bus lines will be moved onto the bridge from 1 March.

A study by the Example Valley University found that traffic in the old town centre fell by 30 percent in the first month after a similar bridge opened upstream in 2019. Shop owners in the centre are less sure: several of them told this paper that they worry fewer people will walk past their windows.

The ferry, which has crossed the river since 1886, will run for the last time on 28 February. Its two boats will be sold, and the council says the money will go to a new cycle path along the east bank.

Construction began in the spring of 2021 and was delayed twice: first by a flood in 2023 and then by a shortage of steel. The building company says 140 workers were on the site at the busiest time.
