City council approves new bus routes after months of debate

The city council voted 9 to 4 on Tuesday evening to approve a package of six new bus routes, ending a debate that began last spring. The new routes start on 1 March next year.

The plan connects the eastern districts to the hospital and the main railway station, areas that residents say have been poorly served for more than a decade. Tickets will keep the current price until the end of the year.

What changes for riders

  • Route 14 runs every 10 minutes between 6:00 and 20:00.
  • Route 22 replaces the night service and runs hourly.
  • Seniors over 65 ride free on all new routes before 9:00.
"For the first time, a patient in the east can reach the hospital without a car," said councillor Marta Kovar.

Opponents argued that the cost, about 3.2 million euros a year, would force cuts to road repairs. The council promised a review after twelve months, and riders can send comments until 15 January.

What happens next

Timetables will be published on the city website in December. Drivers will be hired over the winter, and a test week with free rides is planned for late February.
