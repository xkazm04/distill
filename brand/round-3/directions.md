## Production and review notes

Generated with the built-in image generation tool using the exact prompts in prompts.md. Exported to 1024x1024 logos and 1920x1080 banners through size-only resampling. All five formats and symbols are distinct; none uses a standalone D mark. All banner taglines are correct. D logo and banner were each regenerated once for ambiguous lettering; the final coil-connected ill remains ambiguous and needs typographic refinement before production. These are raster identity explorations, not vector master artwork. A shows a static instance of a proposed generative system; variable particles feed a fixed condenser silhouette. Small-size silhouettes are bold, but the full D wordmark is not reliably readable at 32 pixels.
