Each icon is a 512 × 512 PNG with a bold mark and no text. Each banner is a 1920 × 1080 PNG using its corresponding mark, the wordmark **Distill**, and the tagline **Only what matters on the page.** Palette codes are the intended design specifications; generated raster colors can vary slightly.

## Generation approach

Created with the built-in image generation tool, one asset per call. Matching banners referenced their direction's generated icon; direction 1's icon referenced its banner. Files were normalized to the delivery dimensions after generation.

Shared prompt: Create a simple geometric, flat-color brand identity for Distill, a Chrome extension and Android app simplifying cluttered pages, foreign-language forms and official letters. Audience: immigrants, older adults and non-native speakers. Tone: calm, trustworthy, clear and slightly warm. Avoid tech and medical imagery, gradients, shadows, tiny text and mockups. Icons: mark only, centered with generous margins and a silhouette legible at 32 px. Banners: matching mark, exact wordmark “Distill” and exact tagline “Only what matters on the page.” with clear typography and ample negative space.

Direction prompts: (1) Pine drop with an ivory horizontal cutout; (2) Three rounded navy paths converging into one ochre line; (3) Terracotta page with an apricot folded corner and one ivory line; (4) Thick plum focus ring with a lilac interior and one central plum line; (5) Olive open doorway with one golden stepping stone.
